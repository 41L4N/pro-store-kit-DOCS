# Pro Store Kit

Pro Store Kit is a component and view ecosystem designed as a high-level foundation for enterprise applications with a **strong corporate identity** and complex data management. Its decoupled component architecture enables safe integration with any API, keeps a clean professional look, and shortens time-to-market with a preconfigured base that meets high security and usability standards.

## 📚 Contents

Shared product-domain reference—data, rules, and scope—for teams to align on without relying on a single code source.

- **Domain entities** with their fields and **business typing** (meaning, requiredness, conceptual constraints).
- **Flows** end-to-end and per module (steps, decisions, states).
- **Product modules** and their functional scope.
- **Agreed behaviour:** rules, exceptions, edge cases, and consistency criteria.

### Frontend

- **Core:** Vue.js 3 (Composition API).
- **UI components:** PrimeVue
- **Icons:** Material Icons (Google Material Design).
- **Language:** TypeScript
- **State management:** Pinia
- **HTTP client:** Axios
- **Internationalization:** vue-i18n
- **Build tool:** Vite
- **Styles:** SASS (BEM); details in [conventions](./conventions).

### Backend

- **Core:** Laravel
- **Auth API:** Laravel Sanctum

### Database

- **Core:** MySQL
